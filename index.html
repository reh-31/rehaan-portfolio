model: all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (q8, ~23 MB) · loads in your browser on first use

    How this works

    On your first query, transformers.js downloads a quantised sentence-embedding model into your browser and runs it with ONNX Runtime (WebAssembly). The page is split into chunks (each role bullet, project, skill group, and qualification), every chunk is embedded once, and your question is ranked by cosine similarity, with a small lexical boost for exact keyword matches. This is the retrieval half of a RAG pipeline: it returns the evidence, not a generated answer, so it can't hallucinate. It uses the same embedding model as Quantum Caffeinated Chaos.
